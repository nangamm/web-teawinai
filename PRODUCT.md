# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teawinai serves two primary groups: local contributors who know places worth sharing, and general travelers looking for credible, practical recommendations in Thailand, with the current product focus centered on Ubon Ratchathani. Local users add, update, and recommend places from lived knowledge. Travelers search, filter, compare, and plan trips by location, category, budget, and number of stops.

Admins and place owners are important secondary users. They maintain place data, review price updates, manage listings, and keep the travel database trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Teawinai helps people discover local travel spots and turn them into usable trip plans. The product exists to make local knowledge easier to contribute, organize, and use, without feeling like a hotel-booking or package-tour app. Success means travelers can quickly find relevant places, understand why they are worth visiting, and plan a trip with confidence, while local contributors and admins can keep information accurate with low friction.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, simple, modern.

The voice should feel clear, useful, and locally grounded. It should avoid exaggerated travel hype and keep the interface focused on helping people choose and plan.

## Anti-references

This should not look or feel like a hotel-booking app. Avoid interfaces dominated by room-search patterns, deal badges, reservation pressure, luxury-travel polish, or generic travel marketplace templates.

Avoid cluttered legacy review-site layouts, overdecorated tourism portals, and designs that make local recommendations feel like advertisements instead of useful community knowledge.

## Design Principles

Show local knowledge as the product's advantage.

Make trip planning feel direct: location, budget, category, and results should stay easy to scan and adjust.

Earn trust through clarity: labels, states, approval flows, and data freshness should be obvious.

Keep admin and owner tools task-focused, consistent, and efficient.

Use modern restraint: enough personality to feel Thai and local, without turning the app into a booking engine or a decorative travel brochure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special accessibility requirements were specified. The baseline is readable Thai typography, strong text contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, mobile-first responsive behavior, visible focus states, understandable form errors, and reduced-motion support for non-essential animation.
